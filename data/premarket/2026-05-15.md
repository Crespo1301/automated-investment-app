Alpaca's `daytrade_count` counts unique **days** with day-trade activity, not individual round-trips (that's why 3, not 7). Your mental model was 1 too aggressive: **only the 5/8 day drops Friday morning → 2/3, not 1/3.** Full PDT reset doesn't happen until 5/21.

### Your PDT mitigation question answered

You asked: *PDT is based on opening AND closing the same positions, not symbols. Can we negate them over time by not opening/closing the same positions, or by closing partials? Right now when we sell we sell all.*

**Short answer: partial closes do NOT help PDT. Only overnight holds do.**

FINRA definition: a day trade is "the purchase and sale (or sale and purchase) of the same security on the same trading day." It is per-security per-day, **not** per-share quantity. So:

- **Partial close:** you bought 10 shares today and sell 5 today → still 1 day trade.
- **Sell-then-rebuy:** also a day trade (what happened with F today — manual_exit at 9:31, autopilot rebuy at 10:16).
- **The only way to avoid the PDT counter incrementing:** hold any same-day buy at least one overnight session before any sell of that ticker.

### What this implies for the autopilot

Looking at your 7 round-trips this rolling window, 4 look autopilot-generated (SPY 5/6, QQQ 5/8, WMT 5/8, NIO 5/13 — all bought and sold within minutes-to-hours same session). **That's a risk-gate bug, not a strategy choice.** Codex should add a hard rule:

> The autopilot SHALL NOT exit a position whose `last_buy_filled_at` is in the current ET session unless `daytrade_count < 3` AND the operator has explicitly enabled day-trading via a config flag (default OFF).

The defrag path is already safe (only flags positions >24h old). The bug is in the strategy-engine exit path.
